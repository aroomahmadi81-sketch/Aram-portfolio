About Me

I am a UX design student with a background in graphic design and visual communication. My interest in design started with aesthetics, but gradually shifted toward understanding how people think, feel, and interact with digital products. I have experience working with both graphic and UX design, combining visual clarity with user-centered thinking. I enjoy creating simple, intuitive solutions and believe that good design should reduce complexity and improve everyday experiences.
